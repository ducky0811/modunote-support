FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

常見問題

關於購買、資料保存與檔案處理，先從這裡找答案。

繁體中文常見問題

免費版可以使用哪些功能？

免費版可建立 1 本筆記本、每本最多 10 頁，並可使用手寫、文字、PDF、可計算表格、四種小工具與單頁模板。

ModuNote Pro 是訂閱嗎？

不是。ModuNote Pro 是永久買斷，不是訂閱。購買後解除筆記本數量與每本頁數限制；實際價格以 App Store 顯示為準。

筆記內容放在哪裡？

內容採本機儲存，位於這台裝置上的 App 容器。ModuNote 沒有自有雲端後端，也不提供內建跨裝置同步。

iCloud 會自動同步筆記嗎？

不會。Apple 的 iCloud 或電腦備份是裝置層級備份，可能依系統設定包含 App 資料，但不等於 ModuNote 的即時跨裝置同步。

為什麼匯出的按鈕或表格不能操作？

匯出結果是靜態 PDF，會保存匯出當下可見的文字、手寫、圖片、PDF 頁面、表格結果及小工具外觀，但不會保留 App 內的互動行為。

某個 App 無法接收分享的 PDF，怎麼辦？

系統分享也依賴接收 App 的分享擴充功能。可先將 PDF 儲存至「檔案」，再從目的 App 內選取該檔案。

刪除 App 後還能找回內容嗎？

不一定。刪除 App 會移除裝置上的 App 資料；能否從 Apple 裝置備份還原，取決於既有備份與系統設定。請定期建立 ModuNote 備份並妥善保存。

需要註冊嗎？有廣告或追蹤嗎？

不需要帳號。ModuNote 沒有廣告或追蹤，也沒有分析 SDK。

Frequently asked questions

What is included for free?

The free version includes one notebook with up to 10 pages, plus handwriting, keyboard text, PDF tools, calculating tables, four widgets, and single-page templates.

Is ModuNote Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes notebook and per-notebook page limits. Local pricing appears in the App Store.

Where is my notebook content stored?

Your content is stored locally in the app container on this device. ModuNote has no proprietary cloud backend and does not provide built-in cross-device sync.

Does iCloud automatically sync my notebooks?

No. An Apple iCloud or computer backup is a device-level backup and may include app data according to system settings. It is not live ModuNote synchronization.

Why are exported widgets not interactive?

The export is a static PDF. It preserves the visible page at export time, but buttons, checkbox changes, and calculations do not remain interactive in a PDF reader.

Why can one destination app not receive my PDF?

System sharing also depends on the receiving app's share extension. Save the PDF to Files first, then select it from inside the destination app.

Can I recover content after deleting the app?

Recovery is not guaranteed. Deleting the app removes local app data. Recovery from an Apple device backup depends on the backup and system settings, so keep a separate ModuNote backup.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. ModuNote has no advertising or tracking and does not include an analytics SDK.

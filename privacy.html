PRIVACY POLICY

隱私權政策

我們以清楚、克制的方式說明 ModuNote 如何處理你的內容與檔案。

生效日期：2026-09-27

摘要

你的內容不會主動傳給我們

  • 不需要建立帳號
  • 沒有廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK
  • 沒有開發者自有雲端後端
  • 筆記內容保存在 App 本機容器

繁體中文

1. 適用範圍

本政策適用於 ModuNote 的 iPhone／iPad App，以及此官方支援網站。

2. App 本機儲存

文字、手寫、表格、圖片、PDF、小工具、模板及相關設定，保存在這台裝置的 App 本機容器。開發者不收集、查看或接收這些筆記內容。

3. 使用者主動進行的檔案操作

只有當你主動匯入、匯出、備份或分享時，檔案才會交由 Apple 系統介面及使用者選擇的目的 App 處理。目的 App 或服務如何處理該檔案，受其各自的條款與隱私政策約束。

4. App 內購買

ModuNote Pro 由 Apple StoreKit 與 App Store 處理。App 只向 Apple 取得商品資訊及購買權益狀態；開發者不取得付款卡號或銀行資料，也不會因購買而收到你的筆記內容。

5. Apple 系統備份

iCloud 或電腦備份是 Apple 提供的裝置層級備份，可能依你的裝置與備份設定包含 App 資料。這不是 ModuNote 的跨裝置同步，開發者也無法控制或保證 Apple 備份的建立與還原結果。

6. 資料刪除

你可以在 App 內刪除頁面、筆記本、模板或其他內容。刪除 App 會移除裝置上的 App 資料；既有系統備份是否仍包含較早資料，取決於 Apple 的備份設定與你保留的備份。重要內容請另外建立並妥善保存 ModuNote 備份。

7. 支援網站

此支援網站不使用 Cookie、分析工具或表單，不載入外部字型、追蹤程式或第三方 JavaScript。點選 Email 連結只會開啟你裝置上的郵件 App；只有在你自行寄出郵件後，信件內容才會傳送至客服信箱。

8. 兒童隱私

ModuNote 是一般筆記與生產力工具，並非以兒童為主要使用者。開發者不會透過 App 主動收集兒童的個人資料。

9. 政策變更

若 App 功能、資料處理方式或使用的服務改變，我們會更新本政策與生效日期。若日後加入帳號、同步、分析、AI 或第三方服務，也會重新檢查 App Store 的隱私揭露。

10. 聯絡方式

對本政策有疑問，請寄信至 stockcatcher.pro@gmail.com。請勿在信件中附上真實筆記內容或其他不必要的私人資料。

SUMMARY

Your content is not sent to us automatically

  • No account is required
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK
  • No proprietary developer cloud backend
  • Notebook content stays in the local app container

English

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the ModuNote app for iPhone and iPad and to this official support website.

2. Local app storage

Text, handwriting, tables, images, PDFs, widgets, templates, and related settings are stored in the local app container on this device. The developer does not collect, view, or receive your notebook content.

3. File actions you initiate

Files are handed to Apple system interfaces only when you choose to import, export, back up, or share. They may then be handled by the destination app you select. That app or service processes the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. In-app purchases

Apple StoreKit and the App Store process ModuNote Pro purchases. The app receives product information and entitlement status from Apple. The developer does not receive your card number or bank details, and a purchase does not send us your notebook content.

5. Apple system backups

An iCloud or computer backup is an Apple device-level backup and may include app data according to your device and backup settings. It is not ModuNote cross-device synchronization. The developer cannot control or guarantee Apple's backup or restoration results.

6. Deleting data

You can delete content inside the app, including pages, notebooks, templates, and other items. Deleting the app removes local app data from the device. Whether an existing system backup still contains older data depends on your Apple backup settings and retained backups. Keep a separate ModuNote backup for important content.

7. Support website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or forms. It loads no external fonts, tracking code, or third-party JavaScript. Selecting an email link opens your mail app; content is sent to support only after you choose to send the message.

8. Children's privacy

ModuNote is a general note-taking and productivity tool and is not directed primarily to children. The developer does not use the app to collect children's personal data.

9. Policy changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's features, data practices, or services change. Adding accounts, synchronization, analytics, AI, or third-party services would also require a new App Store privacy review.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email stockcatcher.pro@gmail.com. Do not include private notebook content or other unnecessary personal information.
